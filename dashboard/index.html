How a match works

Each episode is a shift of N ticks on a shared network
Step 1
Random scenario

Attacker and customer get random addresses. The attack starts at a random tick; some shifts have no attack.

Step 2
Red acts

On its ticks, the attacker (an LLM given only a goal) takes one action against the web services.

Step 3
Blue observes and responds

Every tick the defender reads the IDS and traffic logs, then investigates, declares an incident, blocks, or waits.

Step 4
Score and learn

Outcomes come from ground truth. After each training episode both sides write lessons into their playbooks.
